C. Reading and language focus 12 min

Subject: Extra quantity request – Product X, 850 ml (Nov PO)
Dear Li,

Thank you for flagging this business opportunity. I forwarded your request to add 30,000 units to the November PO to our Demand team straight away. Unfortunately, they have come back to me with a negative answer: the current stock is fully allocated, so we cannot supply any additional quantity this month. The next replenishment is expected around 10 December.

If you can accept a later delivery, I can reserve part of the new batch for you as soon as it is confirmed. Alternatively, if some of the customer demand can be covered by another size, I would be happy to check availability. Would you be able to let me know which option you prefer by Friday?

Best regards,
Marie
  1. Why was the request refused?
  2. When is the next replenishment?
  3. What two alternatives does Marie offer?
  4. What does she ask Li to do, and by when?
  5. Find words or phrases in the email that mean: (a) replied · (b) immediately · (c) set aside for you · (d) another possibility.
A good refusal in four steps: ① Acknowledge (Thank you for…) → ② Explain briefly (the stock is fully allocated…) → ③ Offer an alternative (I can reserve… / Alternatively…) → ④ Agree the next step (Would you be able to let me know… by…?)

D. Grammar review – conditionals and their alternatives 12 min

FormStructureExample
Zero conditional (general rules)if + present, presentIf an order arrives after the cut-off, it goes into the next allocation.
First conditional (real future possibility)if + present, will + verbIf you send your list by Monday, I will load those SKUs first.
Alternatives to ifunless (= if … not) · provided (that) / as long as (only if) · in case (as a precaution) · otherwise (or else, at the start of a clause) · If not, (replaces the whole condition) · Should you… (formal)

Remember: no will after if / unless / as long as / in case in the condition part.

E. Fluency – the diplomatic “no” 15 min

Task 1 – Prepare (5 min)

Situation: your distributor asks for 30,000 extra units that you cannot supply. The next replenishment is in three weeks. Make notes using the 4-step model (acknowledge – explain – alternative – next step). Include one conditional sentence and one word from Part B.

Task 2 – Live Teams Role-Play (6 min)

Teams Live Task Deliver your refusal live to your trainer in 60–90 seconds, as if on a Teams call. Your trainer will play the distributor and push back (e.g., “But this customer is very important…”). Respond diplomatically, hold your position while staying constructive, and reach agreement on an alternative. Then switch roles if time permits.
Useful phrases: I understand your position, but… / That is a fair point; however… / Let me see what I can do. / That works for us.
