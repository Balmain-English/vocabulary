C. Language focus – sounding polite and natural 8 min

“Kindly note…” and “pls kindly”. Kindly is common in international email, but it can sound formal or even slightly bossy to native speakers, and pls kindly doubles the politeness. Better options:
• Giving information: Please note that… / Just to let you know… / I wanted to flag that…
• Making a request: Could you please… ? / It would be great if you could… / I’d be grateful if you could…
• Bad news: Unfortunately… / I’m afraid that… / Sadly, we won’t be able to…

Rewrite these blunt sentences in a polite, professional way.

  1. Send me the list by Monday.
  2. I can’t send the invoice before the pick-up.
  3. The pick-up is on 1st December.
  4. Tell me your priorities now.
  5. You didn’t answer my last email.
